how it works

✅

Tick things off

Quests, recipes, research bundles, collections — every entry has a checkbox or little tags for each ingredient.

📌

Pin what you need

Pin what you're working on and the Pinned tab adds up everything you still have to gather. Pinned story quests move on to the next one by themselves.

🔁

Dailies reset themselves

Daily & weekly lists clear on their own, right on your reset time.

🔒

Private by default

No accounts, no tracking. Progress is saved only in this browser — grab a backup file anytime to move devices.
